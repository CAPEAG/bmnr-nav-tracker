Assumptions & methodology

Data flow

  • ETH & BTC: CoinGecko public API, fetched in your browser on load and every 60 s. If it fails, the page falls back to the last prices saved by the GitHub Action, then to the last announcement's prices.
  • BMNR, ETHA, ORBS: Yahoo Finance quotes saved to data/prices.json by a GitHub Action every ~15 min during US market hours (Mon–Fri). The page re-reads that file every 5 min.
  • Holdings (ETH, staked ETH, BTC, cash, Beast, ORBS stake) come from Bitmine's Monday press release / @BitMNR post and are stored in data/holdings.json.
  • Shares outstanding are not disclosed weekly. We carry a running estimate: last count + estimated ATM issuance − disclosed buybacks (see below).

Weekly estimates

  • Staking rewards (est.) = ETH staked at the start of the week × annual staking yield × 7/365. Yield default: (Bitmine's disclosed 7-day BMNR yield).
  • ETH purchased (est.) = reported change in ETH holdings − estimated staking rewards. Assumption: Bitmine's weekly "acquired" figure equals the change in total holdings and we assume rewards credited to its wallets are inside it.
  • Purchase price: if not disclosed, assumed = average daily ETH price over the week (Mon–Sun ending on the "as of" date).
  • Buyback price: if not disclosed, assumed = average BMNR close over that week's trading days.
  • Implied equity raise (sheet logic) = ETH purchase cost + buyback cost − decrease in cash. If positive, est. ATM shares issued = raise ÷ average BMNR close that week; if negative (cash covered it), no issuance is assumed. Opex, preferred (BMNP) dividends and staking cash flows are ignored.

Valuation & caveats

  • ORBS is the Eightco Holdings (NASDAQ: ORBS) stake — a stock, not the Orbs crypto token. Value = assumed 100,000,000 shares × ORBS price (Bitmine discloses only the $ value).
  • Beast Industries stake is carried at the disclosed $180M (not marked to market).
  • "Cash" = Bitmine's "total cash & marketable securities".
  • Preferred stock (BMNP) and any debt are not deducted from NAV (same as the spreadsheet).
  • The 30/60-day ETHA-ratio Z-scores shown are a static copy from the spreadsheet (they are imported from another sheet and not recomputed here).

Not investment advice. Estimates and assumptions may be wrong; always check Bitmine's filings. Data: CoinGecko (ETH, BTC), Yahoo Finance (BMNR, ETHA, ORBS), Bitmine press releases (PR Newswire, @BitMNR, SEC 8-K).
