Assumptions & methodology

Data flow

  • ETH & BTC: CoinGecko public API, fetched in your browser on load and every 60 s. If it fails, the page falls back to the last prices saved by the GitHub Action, then to the last announcement's prices.
  • BMNR, ETHA, ORBS: Yahoo Finance quotes saved to data/prices.json by a GitHub Action every ~15 min during US market hours (Mon–Fri). The page re-reads that file every 5 min.
  • Holdings (ETH, staked ETH, stated staking yield, BTC, cash & marketable securities, Eightco and Beast stakes, buybacks) are transcribed from every Bitmine Monday release (8-K Ex. 99.1 / PR Newswire / @BitMNR) since July 2026 into data/releases.json; scripts/build-history.mjs rebuilds the weekly history from them.
  • Weekly NAV snapshots use the release's ETH price, BTC (CoinGecko) on the as-of date, the last BMNR close on/before the as-of date, and the reported Eightco stake value.

Share-count reconstruction

  • Anchor: last official count, 603,226,394 shares on July 9, 2026 (10-Q cover). Bitmine has not disclosed ATM sales or a newer count since.
  • Staking rewards (est.) = ETH staked at the previous report × that week's stated 7-day BMNR yield × days/365. Rewards arrive in ETH and are inside reported holdings.
  • ETH bought (est.) = change in ETH holdings − est. rewards, priced at the week's average daily ETH price.
  • Buyback price: disclosed if given; otherwise the week's average BMNR close × a factor (≈1.005) calibrated so all buybacks sum to Bitmine's disclosed $399.6M for 22.9M shares.
  • Implied equity raise = Δcash + ETH cost + buyback cost + est. cash costs (≈$3.26M/wk opex from the 10-Q + $0.65M/wk BMNP preferred dividends) − any disclosed proceeds.
  • Shares issued (est.) = raise ÷ week's average BMNR close (0 if the raise is negative); plus disclosed RSU vesting; minus disclosed buybacks.
  • Range: low = no cash costs and issuance at the week's highest close; high = 2× cash costs and issuance at the week's lowest close.

Valuation & caveats

  • ORBS = Eightco Holdings (NASDAQ: ORBS) stock (not the Orbs crypto token): 100,655,143 shares per Bitmine's 10-Q × live ORBS price.
  • Beast Industries stake is carried at the disclosed $180M. "Cash" = "total cash & marketable securities".
  • BMNP preferred (3.5M shares) and other liabilities are not deducted from NAV.
  • Unknowns that can move the share estimate: actual ETH purchase prices, ATM sale prices, operating costs, marketable-securities price changes, and any undisclosed inflows/outflows. The estimate resets to an official count whenever Bitmine files one (10-K for FY ended Aug 31, 2026 is due soon).
  • Valuation scenario inputs (staking multiple, SG&A, MAVAN) are assumptions carried over from the group's spreadsheet.

Not investment advice. Estimates and assumptions may be wrong; always check Bitmine's filings. Data: CoinGecko (ETH, BTC), Yahoo Finance (BMNR, ETHA, ORBS), Bitmine press releases (PR Newswire, @BitMNR, SEC 8-K).
