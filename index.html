    Accretion check (weekly)

    What accretion means: a capital action is accretive if it raises what each existing share owns, and dilutive if it lowers it. Selling new shares above NAV per share (mNAV > 1) leaves more NAV behind each share; selling below NAV (mNAV < 1) does the opposite. Buying back stock below NAV is accretive. Staking rewards add ETH without adding shares, so they are always accretive; cash costs and paying above market for ETH are dilutive. All figures are est. — they use the v2 model's estimated weekly ATM issuance.

    Assumptions & methodology

    Data flow

    • ETH & BTC: CoinGecko public API, fetched in your browser on load and every 60 s. If it fails, the page falls back to the last prices saved by the GitHub Action, then to the last announcement's prices.
    • BMNR, ETHA, ORBS: Yahoo Finance quotes saved to data/prices.json by a GitHub Action every ~15 min during US market hours (Mon–Fri). The page re-reads that file every 5 min.
    • Holdings (ETH, staked ETH, stated staking yield, BTC, cash & marketable securities, Eightco and Beast stakes, buybacks, disclosed investments) are transcribed from every Bitmine Monday release since Nov 30, 2025 (8-K Ex. 99 / @BitMNR) into data/releases.json. Official share counts and ATM sales come from the 10-Qs (data/checkpoints.json).
    • Weekly NAV snapshots use the release's ETH price, BTC (CoinGecko) on the as-of date, the last BMNR close on/before the as-of date, and the reported Eightco stake value.

    Share-count reconstruction (model v2)

    • Anchors: official 10-Q counts — 408.58M (Nov 30, 2025), 454.86M (Jan 12), 493.91M (Feb 28), 579.65M (May 31), 603,226,394 (Jul 9, 2026, latest). Between them the count is rolled forward weekly.
    • Staking rewards (est.) = ETH that was already staked 4 reports earlier × the week's stated 7-day BMNR yield × days/365 (validator entry-queue lag; fitted to 10-Q staking revenue). Rewards arrive in ETH and are inside reported holdings.
    • ETH bought (est.) = change in ETH − est. rewards, costed at the week's average daily ETH price × 1.05 (cash actually paid per the 10-Q cash-flow statements).
    • Buyback price: disclosed if given; otherwise the week's average BMNR close × a factor (≈1.005) calibrated so all buybacks sum to the disclosed $399.6M for 22.9M shares.
    • Cash need = Δcash + ETH cost + buybacks + disclosed investments + operating cash burn ($2.28M/wk, Dec–May 10-Q operating cash flow) + BMNP dividends ($0.65M/wk from Jun 10) − disclosed non-common proceeds.
    • Shares issued (est.) = cash need ÷ week's average BMNR close. A week with surplus cash carries forward to offset later weeks. Plus disclosed RSU/acquisition shares, minus disclosed buybacks.
    • Range: low = no operating cash costs and issuance at the week's highest close; high = 2× costs at the week's lowest close.

    Valuation & caveats

    • ORBS = Eightco Holdings (NASDAQ: ORBS) stock (not the Orbs crypto token): 100,655,143 shares per Bitmine's 10-Q × live ORBS price.
    • Beast Industries stake is carried at the disclosed $180M. "Cash" = "total cash & marketable securities" (includes cash posted as option collateral).
    • BMNP preferred (3.5M shares) and other liabilities are not deducted from NAV.
    • Backtest: v2 is within ~0.1–0.3% of every clean official count since Nov 2025 (see above). Biggest remaining unknowns: within-week timing of ATM sales, rounded cash figures in some releases ($1.0B/$1.2B), ETH execution prices and option-strategy cash flows. The estimate re-anchors whenever Bitmine files a new count (10-K for FY ended Aug 31, 2026 is due).
    • Valuation (Bear/Base/Bull): SOTP = live NAV/share + capitalized net staking/treasury earnings (after company cash opex, BMNP dividends and tax) + MAVAN third-party staking value. Inputs and sources: data/valuation.json (staked share and trailing yields refresh weekly). Options income and interest are excluded.
    • Accretion check: weekly per-share effect of ATM issuance, buybacks, staking and costs, in $ NAV/share and ETH/share, from the v2 model's estimated issuance (see the Accretion section).
