      Accretion check (weekly)

      NAV per share effect ($) est.

      Each week's bars show how much Bitmine's own actions changed NAV per share in dollars: ATM share sales, buybacks, staking rewards and other costs (cash opex, preferred dividends, ETH bought above the week's average price), with ETH, BTC and ORBS price moves left out. Bars above zero added value to every existing share (accretive) and bars below zero took value away (dilutive); the gold line (right axis) is the running total over the selected range.

      ETH per 1,000 shares effect est.

      The same weekly effects measured in ETH owned per 1,000 BMNR shares, so ETH price swings drop out: capital actions (ETH the week's cash could buy minus dilution from new shares), staking rewards, ETH paid above the average price and 10-Q share-count true-ups. Bars above zero mean each share's ETH backing grew that week and bars below zero mean it shrank; the gold line (right axis) is the cumulative change over the selected range.

      Model accuracy / backtest

      Out-of-sample: each interval starts at an official count, rolls the weekly estimator forward and compares with the next filing. Error = estimate − official; bias = mean signed error. "Clean intervals" exclude the Apr 13, 2026 10-Q cover count, which contradicts the same filing (Feb 28 count 493,905,227 + 57,107,586 ATM shares sold through Apr 14 = 551.0M, not 537.6M) — the Feb 28 → May 31 quarter is used instead. v2 and v3 use 10-Q data from Dec 2025 – May 2026 (v2's 1.05 ETH factor, v3's quarterly ETH price and cash-timing items), so their Dec–May rows are partly in-sample; v3 walk-forward only uses 10-Qs filed before each interval starts. The next true test is Bitmine's next official count (10-K for FY ended Aug 31, 2026).

      What Bitmine paid per ETH (10-Q)

      Paid per ETH = cost of ETH purchased ÷ ETH units purchased, from each 10-Q's digital-asset roll-forward (cost basis and units). Market avg = the weekly average ETH price weighted by the ETH added in each weekly release. Bitmine has paid within about ±1% of the market average every quarter, with no trend as purchases got smaller; the 1.05 factor used before came from cash-flow timing (Sep–Nov purchases paid in December, and $112M of unexplained Mar–May digital-asset cash), which v3 adds separately as "other digital-asset cash (10-Q)" in those quarters only. On-chain tracker posts (Lookonchain, OnchainLens, EmberCN: 50 Bitmine-linked transfers since Dec 2025; since Jul 9, 2026, 10 transfers in 8 of 16 weeks, matching the week's reported purchase within ±20% in only 2) date settlement withdrawals and value them at spot; using them made the quarterly ETH cost less accurate, so they are shown only as a cross-check in the data table.

      Assumptions, methodology & notes

      Data flow

      • ETH & BTC: CoinGecko public API, fetched in your browser on load and every 60 s. If it fails, the page falls back to the last prices saved by the GitHub Action, then to the last announcement's prices.
      • BMNR, ORBS: Yahoo Finance quotes saved to data/prices.json by a GitHub Action every ~15 min during US market hours (Mon–Fri). The page re-reads that file every 5 min.
      • Holdings (ETH, staked ETH, stated staking yield, BTC, cash & marketable securities, Eightco and Beast stakes, buybacks, disclosed investments) are transcribed from every Bitmine Monday release since Nov 30, 2025 (8-K Ex. 99 / @BitMNR) into data/releases.json. Official share counts and ATM sales come from the 10-Qs (data/checkpoints.json).
      • Weekly NAV snapshots use the release's ETH price, BTC (CoinGecko) on the as-of date, the last BMNR close on/before the as-of date, and the reported Eightco stake value.

      Share-count reconstruction (model v3)

      • Anchors: official 10-Q counts — 408.58M (Nov 30, 2025), 454.86M (Jan 12), 493.91M (Feb 28), 579.65M (May 31), 603,226,394 (Jul 9, 2026, latest). Between them the count is rolled forward weekly.
      • Staking rewards (est.) = ETH that was already staked 4 reports earlier × the week's stated 7-day BMNR yield × days/365 (validator entry-queue lag; fitted to 10-Q staking revenue). Rewards arrive in ETH and are inside reported holdings.
      • ETH bought (est.) = change in ETH − est. rewards.
      • ETH price paid, with its basis per week: disclosed = inside a quarter with a filed 10-Q, the week's average ETH price × that quarter's paid ÷ market ratio (10-Q cost of ETH purchased ÷ units; 0.998, 0.991, 1.006 for Sep–Nov, Dec–Feb, Mar–May); assumed = after the last 10-Q, the week's average × the pooled ratio (0.998), ±1%. On-chain tracker prices are recorded as a cross-check only (they were less accurate in the backtest).
      • Other digital-asset cash (10-Q): cash paid for digital assets beyond the cost of ETH added — $125M in Dec–Feb (Sep–Nov purchases paid later) and $112M in Mar–May — spread over that quarter's weeks by ETH bought. None assumed after May 31, 2026.
      • Buyback price: disclosed if given; otherwise the week's average BMNR close × a factor (≈1.005) calibrated so all buybacks sum to the disclosed $399.6M for 22.9M shares.
      • Cash shortfall = spending (ETH + other digital-asset cash + buybacks + disclosed investments + operating cash burn $2.28M/wk + BMNP dividends $0.65M/wk from Jun 10) − cash drop − disclosed non-common proceeds.
      • Funded by share sales (est.) = shortfall − surplus carried from prior weeks. A negative shortfall (surplus) is carried for at most 3 weeks and $5M in total (cash in releases is rounded to $1M); anything older or larger is dropped. Shares issued (est.) = funded amount ÷ the week's average BMNR close; plus disclosed RSU/acquisition shares, minus disclosed buybacks.
      • Low–high est. (shares issued, shares outstanding, funded amount): low = no operating cash costs, ETH price 1% below the assumption and issuance at the week's highest close; high = 2× operating costs, ETH price 1% above and the week's lowest close. Weeks with a disclosed (10-Q) ETH price use no ETH-price spread. Ranges accumulate from the last official count.

      Valuation & caveats

      • ORBS = Eightco Holdings (NASDAQ: ORBS) stock (not the Orbs crypto token): 100,655,143 shares per Bitmine's 10-Q × live ORBS price.
      • Beast Industries stake is carried at the disclosed $180M. "Cash" = "total cash & marketable securities" (includes cash posted as option collateral).
      • BMNP preferred (3.5M shares) and other liabilities are not deducted from NAV.
      • Backtest: v3 is within 0.07–0.27% of every clean official count since Nov 2025 (see the backtest section). Biggest remaining unknowns: within-week timing of ATM sales, rounded cash figures (nearest $1M since Jan 4, 2026; $1.0B/$1.2B in some earlier releases), cash-settlement timing of ETH purchases and option-strategy cash flows. The estimate re-anchors whenever Bitmine files a new count (10-K for FY ended Aug 31, 2026 is due).
      • Valuation (Bear/Base/Bull): SOTP = live NAV/share + capitalized net staking/treasury earnings (after company cash opex, BMNP dividends and tax, only if positive) + MAVAN third-party staking value (own tax rate) + a one-off NOL tax shelter (est., pending FY2026 10-K). Inputs and sources: data/valuation.json (staked share and trailing yields refresh weekly). Options income and interest are excluded.
      • Accretion check: weekly per-share effect of ATM issuance, buybacks, staking and costs, in $ NAV/share and ETH/share, from the v3 model's estimated issuance (see the Accretion section).

      • Weekly history: every row is one Bitmine weekly release (8-K Ex. 99 · @BitMNR) since Nov 30, 2025. Green dots = weeks re-anchored to an official 10-Q count; red diamond = the flagged Apr 13 cover count. Bars = week-over-week % change (positive = ETH grew faster than the share count).
      • Accretion: a capital action is accretive if it raises what each existing share owns. Selling shares above NAV/share (mNAV > 1) is accretive, below NAV dilutive; buybacks below NAV are accretive; staking rewards add ETH without new shares; cash costs and paying above market for ETH are dilutive.
      • Valuation: SOTP per share = live NAV per share + capitalized value of the staking/treasury business (net of company cash costs, BMNP preferred dividends and tax, only if positive) + MAVAN's third-party staking business (own tax rate) + a one-off NOL tax shelter (est., pending FY2026 10-K). Inputs and sources: data/valuation.json; staked ETH and trailing yields refresh weekly.
